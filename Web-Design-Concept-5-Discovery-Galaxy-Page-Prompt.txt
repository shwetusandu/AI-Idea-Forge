Design a modern, premium, futuristic SaaS web application page called "Discovery Galaxy" for an AI-powered product discovery platform named "AI Idea Forge".

The page should NOT look like a traditional dashboard.

Instead, it should feel like users are entering an AI Innovation Lab where multiple AI agents collaborate in real time to validate a product idea.

The experience should feel immersive, interactive, and visually impressive while remaining clean and professional.

-----------------------------------------

-----------------------------------------

- Subtle cosmic atmosphere

-----------------------------------------
DESIGN GOAL
-----------------------------------------

The user should feel like they are watching multiple AI experts collaborate around their product idea in real time.

It should feel like:

Apple + Linear + Vercel + Perplexity + Figma

NOT Power BI

NOT Jira

NOT Traditional Admin Dashboard

The page should immediately impress judges and recruiters while remaining clean, modern, intuitive, and production-ready.

Prioritize whitespace, visual hierarchy, premium aesthetics, and immersive interaction over excessive data density.

IMPORTANT:

Do not create unnecessary pages or navigation.

Focus only on the Discovery Galaxy page.

Maintain generous spacing and a premium SaaS appearance.

The visualization should occupy approximately 70% of the page width.

Avoid excessive cards, tables, or text.

Every UI element should support the storytelling that AI agents are collaboratively forging a product idea into a validated product blueprint.
